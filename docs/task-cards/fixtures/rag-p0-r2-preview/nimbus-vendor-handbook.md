# NIM-882 Vendor Onboarding Handbook

## Risk intake

Every NIM-882 vendor submits an Amber Attestation before procurement review. Procurement lead Sofia Ramos assigns the risk tier within three business days. Vendors handling customer content also provide a current penetration-test summary.

## Security review

High-risk vendors complete a Lantern architecture review and name an incident contact. The review must cover encryption, tenant isolation, deletion behavior, and subcontractor access.

## Payment activation

Payment activation occurs only after the vendor record has both an approved risk tier and a signed tax profile. Finance records the activation in the Copper Book. Expedited payment does not bypass security review.

## Renewal

Vendor approval is renewed every twelve months. A material ownership change triggers a new Amber Attestation within ten business days. Expired vendors are blocked from new purchase orders.
