# ORION-417 Release Policy

## Production approval

Project ORION-417 may enter production only after the Cobalt Safety Council records an approval in the Atlas ledger. Release owner Mira Chen must attach the sapphire checklist and a dated rollback rehearsal. The stability observation window is fourteen consecutive days.

## Emergency exception

An ORION-417 emergency exception lasts at most thirty-six hours. It requires two independent reviewers, incident commander Noah Li, and a quartz exception seal. An emergency exception cannot waive the rollback rehearsal.

## Rollback evidence

Rollback evidence must be uploaded to the Atlas ledger within twenty-four hours after the rehearsal. The record includes the deployment hash, the restored database checkpoint, and the name of the on-call verifier.

## Regional rollout

The Shanghai region opens first at ten percent traffic. Singapore follows only after six hours without a severity-one alert. Frankfurt is held until the privacy reviewer signs the regional appendix.

## Change freeze closeout

After Frankfurt opens, the ORION-417 release owner sends a closeout packet to assurance coordinator Priya Sen within two business days. The packet is stored in the Obsidian Docket and contains the regional approval timestamps plus the final traffic allocation. Production changes stay frozen until Priya records receipt.
