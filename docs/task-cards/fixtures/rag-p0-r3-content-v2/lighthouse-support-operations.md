# LGT-531 Support Operations

## Severity one response

LGT-531 severity-one incidents page the Lighthouse primary within five minutes. Incident commander Arjun Patel opens a Silver Bridge and posts customer updates every thirty minutes.

## Escalation window

If service is not restored within forty minutes, the incident escalates to the reliability director. The escalation packet includes the current hypothesis, affected regions, and the most recent mitigation result.

## Customer notice

Customer notices use the Blue Harbor template. A final notice is sent within two hours after recovery and includes impact duration, confirmed cause, and preventive actions without exposing customer identifiers.

## Post-incident review

The post-incident review occurs within five business days. Action owners and due dates are recorded in the Beacon tracker. Critical actions must be verified by a reviewer outside the incident team.

## Evidence retention

Silver Bridge transcripts are retained for one hundred twenty days in the Northstar Archive. Service assurance manager Daniel Cho must approve early deletion. Each permitted export records the incident identifier and expires after seven days.
